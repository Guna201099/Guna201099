## Guna Thiagarajan

Sensors, test benches and machine learning. Dortmund, Germany.

**Portfolio with video &rarr; [guna201099.github.io](https://guna201099.github.io)**

I build the part between an instrument and a model: getting a clean signal off a
machine, then writing the software that makes sense of it.

Right now that is a live dashboard for a CO&#8322; electrolysis rig at **Fraunhofer
UMSICHT**, my master's thesis. It reads the rig while the experiment is still
running and tells the scientists how it is going, instead of leaving them to work
it out in Excel afterwards. Getting a test campaign ready went from hours to
about five minutes.

Before that I spent eighteen months at **HARMAN International** on the
infotainment computers in BMW and Mercedes cars, where the open warranty backlog
went from 123 cases into single digits in six months.

Earlier I built instruments that had to survive real use. A point-of-care device
that reads heart rate and blood oxygen. A deionised water dispenser that is still
feeding CNC machines. A hospital bed that a wheelchair drives out of. Two were
sold to customers and two were published.

**Python, scikit-learn, DuckDB, SQL, PyQt6, LabVIEW, MATLAB, Embedded C, OPC UA,
hardware-in-the-loop testing.**
