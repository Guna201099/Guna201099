### Now

M.Sc. Sustainable Energy Systems at TU Dortmund, finishing October 2026.

My thesis at Fraunhofer UMSICHT is a live dashboard for a CO&#8322; electrolysis rig.
It reads the machine while the experiment is still running, repairs and time
aligns every sensor channel into DuckDB at the edge, watches for the point where
the readings change shape, and forecasts against what the rig did before.
Getting a test campaign ready went from hours to about five minutes.

### Before that

At HARMAN International I took the open warranty backlog on the BMW and Mercedes
Hermes infotainment programme from 123 cases into single digits in six months,
using 8D root-cause analysis and hardware-in-the-loop validation. HARMAN
recognised the result in November 2023.

Earlier I built instruments: a point-of-care device that reads heart rate and
blood oxygen without the processor ever holding the sensor up, a deionised water
dispenser that is still feeding CNC machines, and a hospital bed that a
wheelchair drives out of. Two were sold to customers. Two are published.
